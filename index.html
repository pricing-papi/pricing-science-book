Excerpt · Principle one

The biggest financial loss in your company is missing from the P&L.

Imagine your company charged $10,000 for a product last year. You can see exactly what happened at $10,000. What you cannot see is what would have happened at $15,000.

Income statementOne sale
Price charged$10,000
Revenue recorded$10,000
StatusSuccessful sale ✓
What the customer would have paid$15,000
Revenue never captured($5,000)
Nothing in normal reporting tells anyone something went wrong.

Nobody sees the missing $5,000 because the transaction still happened. The salesperson gets credit, revenue increases, and the customer begins using the product.
